# Magic Forest: Apprentice Wizard - Game Design Document

## I. Game Overview

### 1.1 Basic Information

| Item | Content |
|------|---------|
| Game Name | Magic Forest: Apprentice Wizard |
| Game Type | 3D Adventure Puzzle / RPG |
| Development Platform | Unity |
| Target Platform | PC |
| Game Duration | Approximately 2-4 hours |

### 1.2 Game Introduction

*Magic Forest: Apprentice Wizard* is an action-puzzle game centered around forest exploration. Players will take on the role of an apprentice mage new to the magical world, exploring the mysterious Elwyn Forest, solving puzzles, helping others, and collecting five lost spellbook pages. Ultimately, players will understand the true meaning of magic and become a qualified mage.

The game emphasizes that "magic is not in books, but in the moment you are willing to help others" — by helping the forest residents, players will gradually understand the five core qualities of a mage: courage, wisdom, tolerance, perseverance, and kindness.

---

## II. Story Background

Long ago, there was a forgotten forest called Elwyn. The leaves here glow, the streams sing, and mushrooms strike up conversations. A legendary witch named Granny Meriel lived in this forest. She poured all her knowledge into a thick spellbook, but felt that "learning it all at once would be no fun," so she tore the book into five pages and hid them throughout the forest.

The Dark King covets the forest's power and constantly sends troops to invade. The barrier around Elwyn Forest is weakening.

You are an ordinary person, not a chosen one. You enter this forest, searching for the five lost pages, helping the people and animals you meet along the way, and ultimately discovering: true magic is never in books.

## IV. Core Gameplay

### 4.1 Spellbook Page Collection System

Players need to complete five chapters to collect five spellbook pages. Each page is obtained in a different way:

| Page | Acquisition Method |
|------|-------------------|
| Page 1 | Complete altar stone puzzle, save the fairy |
| Page 2 | Complete maze test, answer at least 8 of the guard's 10 quality questions correctly |
| Page 3 | Collect all four colored keys, open the locked house |
| Page 4 | Help Luna the fairy find her lost key |
| Page 5 | Understand the true meaning of magic at the Moonlit Glade |

### 4.2 Four Key System (Chapter 3)

Players need to collect four colored keys to open the locked house:

| Key | Color | Acquisition Method |
|-----|-------|-------------------|
| Yellow Key | Yellow | Help bear find silver herb, exchange for honey from baker |
| Red Key | Red | Complete piano note memory test |
| Green Key | Green | Complete card memory game |
| Blue Key | Blue | Complete color square survival game (5 levels) |

### 4.3 Quality Test System (Chapter 2)

The game emphasizes five qualities of becoming a mage:

| Quality | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Courage | Don't back down when facing difficulties |
| Wisdom | Good at observing and thinking |
| Tolerance | Understand others' difficulties |
| Perseverance | Don't give up when encountering setbacks |
| Kindness | Willing to help others |

Players encounter information about these qualities during maze exploration. Ultimately, they must answer at least 8 of the guard's 10 related questions correctly to obtain the second page.

### 4.4 Moral Choice System (Chapter 4)

During the old tree trial, players face a moral choice:

| Option | Result |
|--------|--------|
| Destroy the cuckoo eggs | Cursed and despised by the old tree |
| Respect nature's laws | Receive reward, trigger side quest |
| Help build a safe nest for the reed warbler | Collect 7 fences + 2 shrub saplings, receive special reward |

## V. Chapter Details

### 5.1 Chapter 1: Magic Forest

**Objective:** Learn basic controls, obtain the first spellbook page

**Process:**
1. Player completes tutorial, following the flower path
2. Discovers mysterious altar, hears crying
3. Encounters trapped fairy (trapped by the Dark King)
4. Needs to find clues in the forest, push altar stones in correct order
5. Saves the fairy, obtains first spellbook page
6. Hears roar, fairy says Dark King is sending troops to attack the forest
7. Player investigates, witnesses guard defeating monsters
8. Player makes a wish to "become a true mage and protect the forest"
9. Guard gives player a portal, enter Chapter 2

**Side Quest: Finding the Fairy's Memory**
- Complete picture puzzle
- Unlock the story of the fairy and her mother

### 5.2 Chapter 2: Forest Maze

**Objective:** Pass the maze test, obtain second spellbook page

**Hidden Clues:** Two posters with the message: "Respect nature, do not interfere with the forest's growth patterns"

**Process:**
1. Player enters the large maze
2. Guard is unfriendly, demands an entry ticket
3. Complete hopscotch mini-game to obtain entry ticket
4. Enter the maze test
5. Maze contains hints about the five qualities
6. After exiting the maze, guard gives 10 questions (about courage, wisdom, tolerance, perseverance, kindness)
7. Answer at least 8 correctly, obtain second spellbook page
8. Barrier falls, Chapter 3 portal opens

### 5.3 Chapter 3: Forest Treehouse

**Objective:** Collect all four colored keys, obtain third spellbook page

**Process:**
1. Talk to passerby at the pavilion, learn about a house locked with four locks
2. Find the four colored keys:

**Yellow Key:**
- Ask the baker, learn there is no honey
- Find the bear in the tree hollow, bear asks for silver herb to treat back pain
- Find silver herb, give to bear, obtain honey
- Give honey to baker, obtain yellow key

**Red Key:**
- Complete piano note memory test
- Listen to the note sequence and play correctly

**Green Key:**
- Complete card memory game with the old man
- Old man shuffles the order of four cards, names one card
- Player selects the correct answer

**Blue Key:**
- Complete five levels of color square survival game
- Countdown timers: 30 sec, 20 sec, 15 sec, 10 sec, 5 sec
- Difficulty increases each level
- Player must stand on the designated color square within the time limit
- After time expires, other colored squares disappear; player falls if on the wrong square

3. Collect all four keys, open the house
4. Obtain third spellbook page from the treasure chest
5. Enter portal to Chapter 4

**Side Quest: Merchant's Trouble**
- Merchant says goods should have arrived at noon but haven't
- Player discovers worker's wooden cart is broken
- Find tools to help repair
- Merchant thanks player, gives glowing berry

### 5.4 Chapter 4: Forest Swamp

**Objective:** Help Luna the fairy, obtain fourth spellbook page

**Process:**
1. Discover Luna crying because she lost her key to return home
2. Find the forest witch for help
3. Witch says her fan is broken, needs a feather fan
4. Find four feathers in the swamp, give to the witch
5. Witch hints: magpies like shiny objects, the key is in the magpie nest at the top of the tree trunk
6. Witch helps set up a ladder
7. Player climbs the ladder, gets the key from the magpie nest
8. Luna's home is a treehouse between two giant trees
9. Player needs to jump on mushrooms to reach it
10. Return the key to Luna, obtain fourth spellbook page

**Side Quest: Old Tree's Trial**
- If player speaks rudely to the old tree, they will be attacked
- Normal interaction: Old tree explains the cuckoo bird's habit of laying eggs in other birds' nests
- Old tree gives options:
  - Take or break the cuckoo eggs → Cursed and despised
  - Respect nature's laws → Receive reward
  - Help build a safe nest for the reed warbler → Collect 7 fences + ask witch for 2 shrub saplings, build a safer nest

### 5.5 Chapter 5: Moonlit Glade

**Objective:** Understand the true meaning of magic, obtain fifth spellbook page

**Process:**
1. Player searches everywhere but cannot find the last page
2. Comes to a mysterious place: sparkling fireflies, floating steps
3. Reaches the end, hears Granny Meriel's voice
4. Player laments that they cannot find the fifth page
5. Granny Meriel says: "You already have it."
6. She lists the qualities the player demonstrated during the journey: courage, kindness, tolerance, perseverance, wisdom
7. She says: "The fifth page is not in any book. It is in every moment you are willing to help others."
8. Player obtains the fifth spellbook page, becomes a true mage
9. Easter egg: The Dark King's spell is about to break through the barrier; Elwyn Forest is in peril
10. Teaser: *Magic Forest: Forest Defense Battle*
