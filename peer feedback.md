Peer Feedback

Strength
A map has been added to the game, which helps players understand the overall layout and find their way around more easily.

Risk
The map is not detailed enough. It lacks clear markers for key locations such as the seven houses, the starting point, and the final area. Players may still feel confused about where to go next. Also, there is no sound in the game at the moment. No background music, no sound effects for interactions, and no feedback sounds for completing tasks. This makes the world feel less alive and reduces immersion.

Question about contribution
Do you plan to use free sound effect libraries, or will you record or synthesize the audio yourself?

My answer
I placed the main landmarks on the map, but I wasn't sure how much detail to add without making it look too crowded. For audio, I plan to use free sound effect libraries and royalty-free background music for now, rather than recording or synthesizing my own.

Practical suggestion
Add at least simple icons or labels on the map for the seven houses and the player's current position. Also, add basic background music and a few sound effects (like picking up an item, completing a task, or opening a door). Even simple sounds will greatly improve the feeling of the game.
