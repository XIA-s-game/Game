There is a place kissed by sunlight and cradled by gentle breezes.
They call it the Forest of Elwyn.
Here, leaves shimmer with their own light. Streams hum old melodies. Mushrooms stop you for a chat—though most of what they say is nonsense, and that is precisely the charm of it.
Elwyn is no place of danger. It is more like a great, rambunctious, tenderhearted school of magic—where classrooms hide inside hollow trees, and textbooks grow upon the branches.
Long, long ago, a most extraordinary witch lived here. They called her Granny Meriel.
She poured everything she knew into a single book. But she thought to herself: "Learning it all at once… where is the joy in that?"
So she tore her book into five pages, tucked them away in the forest’s quietest corners, and left behind a small note:
“So you wish to be a mage?
Then find these five pages.
One page, one spell.
Find them all, and you shall be.
— Granny Meriel”
And you—you are the next one who comes looking for those pages.
No chosen one. No hero of legend.
Just someone who thought, "Being a mage would be rather wonderful."
So you sling your bag across your shoulder, pack a sandwich or two, and step into the trees.
