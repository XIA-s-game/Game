Game Overview:
A story about an apprentice mage exploring the Elwyn Forest, collecting five pages of a spellbook, and ultimately becoming a true mage.

About the Video in the Game:
The images used in the video were generated by me using AI image generation tools. The video was edited by myself.

About the Assets in the Game:
I downloaded an asset pack from the Unity Asset Store. The flowers, trees, and houses in the game come from this asset pack. The remaining models are sourced from various public free websites (I will provide a complete list later) and were built by myself.
